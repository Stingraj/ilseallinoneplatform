Ilse Sørensen Murdock

Born in Copenhagen, Ilse Sørensen Murdock works between her studio in Berlin and the coast of Jutland. Her practice spans painting, drawing, and site-specific installation, grounded in a decades-long meditation on light, surface, and silence.

After studying at the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts, she spent formative years in the studio of Per Kirkeby, absorbing a Nordic sensibility that privileges restraint over declaration. Her work has been shown at Kunsthal Charlottenborg, Den Frie, and Galerie Nordenhake.

Each piece begins with observation — the way winter light falls across plaster, the trace of salt on linen, the moment before a form resolves. What remains is never explanation, only presence.
